Settings and help

How this works

  • Your gem anchor is the most recent count you logged with Log your gems. The plan counts forward from that date, and a banner that had already started on that day is treated as paid for. Until you log one, the starting count above is used. Fix or delete an old count in the Gem log.
  • Units is how many banners you want to spend on: 0 to skip, 1 or 2 (or more) otherwise. Each costs the default cost above, the worst case.
  • Income is estimated from your gem log: how much your count rose between logs, plus the units you spent in between at the default cost, averaged over the lookback above. Each banner then earns that rate for its days. Units you spent but did not enter make it read low.
  • Expected after is anchor + income so far − planned spend so far. Red means it would go below zero.
  • Lowest ahead looks all the way to the end of your plan. At or above 0 means everything you have planned fits. The row marked Lowest point is the one that limits what more you can plan.
  • Room for extra? appears when that lowest point still covers another unit.
  • Banners come from the Global forecast and follow its dates. Type Units on the ones you want, or use − and +. Ended banners move to the Archive when you open the page.
